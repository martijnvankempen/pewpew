# Pew Pew!

Pew the cat-octopus fires spinning stars at a marching alien fleet.

## Usage

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| LEFT / RIGHT | Move Pew |
| SELECT | Fire a star (hold for auto-fire); start, resume, play again |
| UP | Octo-burst: a fan of seven stars, once the charge bar is full |
| MENU or BACK | Pause; BACK again returns to the title |
| DOWN (title screen) | Toggle tilt-to-steer |
| HOME | Back to the badge menu |

Pew fires from alternating finger-guns, up to four stars at a time. Every
alien hit charges the Octo-burst; ten hits fill the bar along the bottom of
the screen. Stars also cancel alien zaps. Clear all 15 aliens to start a
faster wave; you lose when your three lives are gone or the fleet reaches Pew.

Aliens are worth 30 (top row), 20 (middle) and 10 (bottom) points, double
when diving, and clearing a wave adds 100.

The fleet bobs, changes pace and sometimes doubles back. Aliens shiver before
they break formation to swoop at Pew, firing aimed zaps on the way down; a
diver that misses loops back in from the top. Later waves march faster, fire
more often and send more divers.

With tilt-to-steer on, the way you hold the badge when a game starts counts as
level; lean it about 15 degrees left or right for full speed. The case LEDs
flash on hits and chase around the badge during an Octo-burst.

Your best score (saved at game over) and the tilt setting are kept on the
badge. No Wi-Fi or extra hardware is needed.
